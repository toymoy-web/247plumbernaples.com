Slab Leak Repair in Naples, FL

A warm patch on the floor, a hissing sound under the concrete, or a water bill that keeps climbing usually means a slab leak. Most Naples homes are built on a slab, so this is one of our most common calls, especially in homes built before the 1990s.

Warm Spot On The Floor? Here's What That Usually Means

A warm or damp patch of tile or carpet, especially near a bathroom or the kitchen, is one of the clearest signs of a hot water line leaking under the concrete slab. It's not something you can patch from above. Confirming and fixing a slab leak means locating the exact point of the leak first, then choosing the least disruptive way to reach it.

Why This Happens In Naples

Sandy Soil, Older Copper, And Slab Foundations

Naples sits on sandy, sometimes shifting soil, which puts uneven pressure on the copper supply lines that run under most slab foundations. Combine that with the age of copper piping in homes built before the 1990s, and slab leaks become one of the more predictable plumbing problems in this area rather than a rare event. Homes on the coast also see faster pipe wear from humidity and, indirectly, from the salt content in the air working into any exposed fittings near the slab edge.

Signs To Watch For

How A Slab Leak Shows Up

🌡️

Warm Floor Spot

Usually the hot water line, this is the single most reported sign.

🔊

Hissing Under The Floor

Audible in quiet rooms, especially at night when household noise is lower.

💵

Rising Water Bill

A leak running continuously under the slab adds up fast on a monthly bill.

🏚️

Cracked Or Buckling Flooring

In advanced cases, sustained moisture under tile or laminate can cause visible damage.

Repair Options

How We Fix A Confirmed Slab Leak

Once the leak is located, there are generally two paths. A spot repair opens a small, targeted section of slab directly over the leak, the fastest and least expensive option for a single, isolated leak. A reroute runs a new line above the slab or through the attic, bypassing the damaged section entirely, often the better choice for an older home where one slab leak is likely to be followed by another nearby. We'll walk you through both with real numbers before you decide.

Related Services

Before And After A Slab Leak

Every slab leak repair starts with leak detection to confirm the exact location before any concrete is opened. If your home has had more than one slab leak, ask about whole-home repiping as a longer-term fix instead of repeated spot repairs. And if water has already reached your flooring or drywall, our water damage restoration service can be scheduled for the same visit.

Every slab leak repair we perform follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, the statewide standard licensed plumbers work under for pipe material, joining methods, and pressure testing after a repair. That last step matters most here: we pressure test the repaired section before closing it back up, so you're not left wondering whether the fix actually held.

After The Repair

Restoring The Slab And Flooring

A spot repair leaves a patched section of slab that needs to cure before flooring goes back down, typically a few days depending on the patch size and material. We'll coordinate the timing with you so the area is ready for tile, laminate, or carpet to be reinstalled, and we can point you toward flooring contractors we've worked with before if you don't already have one. If the leak caused enough water intrusion to affect drywall or baseboards beyond the immediate repair area, that's a conversation for our water damage restoration team rather than something to leave unaddressed.

Where Slab Leaks Hit

Naples Manor, Lely, And The Rest Of The Slab Belt

Slab leaks are a countywide issue, but they cluster in neighborhoods with decades on the same lines. Naples Manor and Lely, both about five miles from downtown on US 41, and the older parts of East Naples are typical. In Old Naples and Royal Harbor, older homes remodeled on top of original slabs can hide old lines under new flooring.

Multi-story buildings in Park Shore and Pelican Bay are a different case, because a leak on the ground floor may come from a shared riser and not from a pipe in the slab, and that changes who has to be involved in the repair.

Common Questions

Slab Leak FAQ

How do you find a slab leak without tearing up my whole floor?

We use acoustic and thermal detection equipment to pinpoint the leak's location first, so any opening we make for the repair is small and targeted.

Is a spot repair or a reroute better?

It depends on the home's age and pipe condition. We'll explain the honest trade-off for your specific situation rather than defaulting to one option.

Will homeowners insurance cover a slab leak?

Coverage varies by policy. Many cover resulting water damage but not the plumbing repair itself. Check with your insurer before the visit.

How do I know if it's a slab leak and not just a humid spot?

A true slab leak spot stays consistently warm or damp regardless of weather or air conditioning use, while ordinary humidity fluctuates with conditions. If you're not sure, an electronic leak survey settles the question quickly.
