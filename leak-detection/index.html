Water Bill Up And Nothing Obvious Is Wrong?

That combination, a higher bill with no new leaks in sight, is the single most common reason Naples homeowners call us. Most of the time the water is running somewhere you can't see it: under the slab, inside a wall, or along a buried supply line between the meter and the house. Electronic leak detection is how we find that spot without opening up the whole floor to look for it.

Why This Happens Here

Naples' Slab Foundations Hide Leaks Well

Most homes in Naples and across Collier County sit on a concrete slab rather than a basement, so a supply line leak under the slab has nowhere obvious to show itself.

Sandy, shifting soil under the slab adds stress to copper piping over time, and in older homes built before the 1990s, that stress plus age is the most common combination behind a slab leak.

Coastal humidity also means a slow leak can run for weeks before a homeowner notices anything beyond a slightly damp patch of carpet or a warmer-than-usual section of tile.

Signs To Watch For

How A Hidden Leak Usually Shows Up

💵

Higher Water Bill

A bill that jumps with no change in household water use is the most reliable early sign.

🌡️

Warm Or Damp Floor Spot

A hot water line leaking under the slab often creates one warm patch of tile or flooring.

🔊

Running Water Sound

Hearing water move through the walls or floor with every fixture in the house shut off.

🌱

Unusually Green Patch Of Lawn

One noticeably greener or wetter area of yard can point to a leaking underground supply line.

How We Find It

Detection Methods We Actually Use

We start with the least invasive option and only escalate if it's genuinely needed.

  • Acoustic listening equipment picks up the sound signature of pressurized water escaping through a pinhole in a pipe, even through concrete.
  • Pressure testing isolates which line, hot or cold, the leak is on before any physical search begins.
  • Thermal imaging can reveal a hot water line leak under a slab as a heat pattern invisible to the eye.
  • Tracer gas testing is used on harder cases, a safe, non-toxic gas is introduced into the line and a sensor traces where it escapes.

In nearly every case, this lets us confirm the leak's location before any concrete or drywall is opened.

Related Services

If The Leak Turns Out To Be...

Detection is the first step. Once we know where the leak is, the fix depends on what we find. If it's under the slab, that usually means slab leak repair. If age and pipe material are the underlying issue across the whole house, we'll talk through whole-home repiping as an option instead of chasing repeated pinhole leaks.

And if standing water has already reached drywall or flooring by the time you call, ask about our water damage restoration service too.

Homeowners in Golden Gate Estates should note that a sudden spike in well pump run time can point to the same kind of hidden leak, just on the supply side of a private well system rather than a municipal line.

Any repair work we perform follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which sets the material and installation standards licensed plumbers work under statewide. That matters most on a repair like this because a spot fix done to code holds up over years, not just until the next inspection, and it keeps the repair from becoming a liability if you sell the home later.

What To Expect

From First Call To Confirmed Location

Most leak detection visits follow the same basic sequence. We start by asking what you've noticed, a bill spike, a sound, a damp spot, since that narrows down which lines to test first. Next comes pressure isolation to confirm whether the leak is on the hot or cold side, then the acoustic or thermal survey to narrow the physical location.

In most residential cases we can point to the leak's location within a few feet, which is what keeps the eventual repair small. We'll show you what we found before recommending anything, not just tell you a number.

When It Cannot Wait

Emergency Leak Detection: When To Call Right Now

Most leak calls start as a question, and some turn into an emergency within the hour. If water is visibly spreading, a ceiling is stained and sagging, or the meter dial is spinning fast with every fixture off, treat it as urgent and call our 24/7 line.

Close the main shutoff at the house before anything else. That stops new water from entering the home while we are on the way, and it tells you something useful. If the meter keeps moving with the main closed, the leak is on the supply side of your shutoff, often in the buried line from the meter to the house.

🚰

Shut Off The Main

Turn the house main valve fully clockwise, then listen for whether the sound of running water stops.

⚡

Keep Clear Of Wet Electrical

Stay away from wet outlets and ceiling fixtures. Switch off the breaker for a wet area only if you can reach it dry.

📸

Record What You See

Photograph stains, wet spots, and the meter reading. It speeds up diagnosis and helps with an insurance claim.

Meter Test

Check Your Water Meter Before You Call

A running meter is the most common reason people ask us for detection. Shut off every faucet, the ice maker, and the irrigation, then look at the meter's small leak indicator or low-flow dial. If it moves while nothing is on, water is escaping somewhere.

Next, close the main valve at the house and look again. A dial that stops points to a leak inside the home. A dial that keeps turning points to the buried line between the meter and the house, which is the pipe most homeowners never see.

In unincorporated Collier County, the county's billing FAQ says the utility is not responsible for leaks on the customer side of the meter or backflow device. Inside the City of Naples, ask the utility where its responsibility ends before you assume the pipe is theirs.

If a utility notice or a high bill is what started this, give us the dates and amounts. A sudden jump usually means a new break, while a slow climb over several months usually means a seep that has been running a while.

Outdoor Lines

Irrigation, Pool, And Hot Tub Supply Leaks

Not every hidden leak sits under the house. Naples landscaping runs on irrigation lines just below the surface, and a cracked fitting can waste a lot of water before anyone notices a soggy patch.

Signs include a zone that runs weakly, a valve box full of water, one oddly green strip of lawn, or irrigation that seems to run fast when a valve opens. Pool, spa, and hot tub supply lines leak the same way, usually with a wet spot near the equipment pad.

We test the supply side first so you learn whether the problem is the irrigation, the pool plumbing, or the house line before anyone digs.

Rentals And Businesses

Leak Detection For Multi-Unit And Commercial Properties

Property managers call about duplexes, triplexes, small apartment buildings, and plaza units where the leak could be in any unit or in a shared line. When a building has several shutoffs, finding the right section of pipe is half the job.

We isolate one line at a time and listen and scan before opening anything, which means less downtime and fewer opened walls. Water standing in a parking lot, an overflowing floor drain, or a utility notice about a constant leak are all reasons to call the same day.

Scope varies by building, so describe the property and we will tell you what the visit involves.

Common Questions

Leak Detection FAQ

My water bill went up but I don't see any leak. What's going on?

This is the most common call we get. In most cases the leak is under the slab or inside a wall where it can't be seen, and electronic detection is how we confirm exactly where before opening anything up.

Do you have to cut into my floor to find a leak?

Not to find it. Acoustic and thermal equipment let us pinpoint the location first, so any opening we make for the repair is targeted rather than exploratory.

How long does leak detection take?

Most residential leak detection visits take one to two hours depending on the size of the home and how many lines need to be tested.

Is leak detection covered by homeowners insurance?

Most policies cover sudden, accidental water damage caused by a leak but not the cost of finding or repairing the leak itself. Check your specific policy or ask your insurer before the visit.

Can you fix the leak once you find it?

Yes. Most callers want the same company to find and repair the leak, and we do both. The repair price depends on where the pipe is and how deep or how hidden it sits.

A plumber already looked and could not find my leak. Can you help?

Often, yes. Visual checks and pressure tests narrow a leak down but can stop short of the exact spot. Acoustic and thermal equipment is built for that last step, and we mark the location before any opening is made.

Is a running water meter an emergency?

If the meter spins fast with everything off, treat it as urgent. Close the main valve, call our 24/7 line, and we will start with whether the loss is inside the home or on the buried supply line.
