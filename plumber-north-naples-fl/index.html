Neighborhoods First

Pelican Bay, Vanderbilt Beach, Naples Park, And What Each One Needs

North Naples is an unincorporated community in northwest Collier County, immediately north of the city of Naples. It takes in the census-designated places of Naples Park, Pelican Bay, and Pine Ridge, plus a large amount of surrounding land that shares a Naples mailing address. For plumbing, it behaves like three different markets sharing one name.

Along the Gulf, Pelican Bay, Bay Colony, and Vanderbilt Beach are built around condominium buildings, where one failed supply line can reach the units below and a building manager has to be part of the fix. Naples Park is a grid of single-family blocks bounded by US 41, 111th Avenue North, Vanderbilt Drive, and 91st Avenue North. Mercato, at Vanderbilt Beach Road and US 41, adds restaurants and retail with their own grease-line and water-heater demands.

Know Your ZIP

Coverage Across 34108, 34109, 34110, And 34119

North Naples spreads across four ZIP codes. The 34108 area holds the beachfront side, including Pelican Bay, Vanderbilt Beach, and Bay Colony. The 34109 area is the central part of the community and its largest by population, at roughly 26,600 residents. The 34110 area reaches north toward the Bonita Springs line, and 34119 covers the Logan Boulevard side near Pine Ridge and Livingston Woods.

Our dispatch also reaches the bordering ZIPs, including 34103 and 34105 to the south, 34116 to the east, and the 34134 area across the Lee County line. If you are not sure which ZIP you are in, give us your cross streets when you call and we will dispatch from there.

Condos And HOAs

Plumbing Work In A Building Is Different

Water shutoffs in a condo building are often shared, so stopping a leak can mean closing a riser that serves several units. We ask early whether the building has an on-site manager or a management company, because they usually hold the keys, the shutoff locations, and the association's rules for contractors.

Many associations also set working hours and want notice before water is shut off. Water heater replacement follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and needs a permit, so the paperwork is part of the job. In a single-family home the sequence is simpler, and you get a written price before any repair starts.

Salt Air And Storm Season

What The Gulf Does To Plumbing On The Beach Side

Buildings within a few blocks of the Gulf deal with the same salt-laden humidity that speeds corrosion on Marco Island, though usually less severely. Hose bibs, exposed fittings, and water heater connections on balconies and in garages are the first to show green or white crusting.

Hurricane Ian in September 2022 knocked out power in Pelican Bay and Bay Colony for several days, and it caused roughly $2.2 billion in damage to buildings across Collier County. Power loss is a plumbing problem too, because pumps stop and electric water heaters go cold. If a pump in a garage pit quits during an outage, our sump pump repair service handles it, and our water damage restoration team responds 24/7 after storms.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Before We Arrive In North Naples

In a condo or a house, the first minutes decide how far the water spreads. Here is what to do while our emergency line dispatches someone to you.

1

Shut Off The Water

Close the unit or main shutoff. In a condo, call building management for the riser valve if the unit valve will not stop the flow.

2

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is reaching an outlet or fixture, shut off power to that area at the breaker box.

3

Warn The Units Below

In a multi-story building, call or knock on the doors below so neighbors can move belongings and check ceilings.

4

Call Our 24/7 Line

Do you cover every ZIP code in North Naples?

Yes, we cover 34108, 34109, 34110, and 34119, from the Pelican Bay and Vanderbilt Beach side to the Logan Boulevard side near Pine Ridge, plus the bordering ZIPs on every side.

Is emergency service available in North Naples?

Yes, our emergency line dispatches to North Naples 24/7, every day of the year, for a burst pipe, a failed water heater, or a backup that cannot wait.

Do you work inside condo buildings?

Yes. We coordinate with building management on shutoffs, access, and association rules, and we explain what we find before any repair starts.

Does a water heater replacement in a condo need a permit?

Generally yes, since water heater replacement falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing. We handle the paperwork as part of the job, and an association may add its own requirements.

Which North Naples neighborhoods do you serve?

Pelican Bay, Bay Colony, Vanderbilt Beach, Naples Park, Pine Ridge, the Logan Boulevard area, and the streets around Mercato, among others.

Can you find a leak I can hear but cannot see?

Often yes. Acoustic and thermal equipment locate leaks inside walls and under slabs without opening them first, and ceiling leaks between condo units are among the most common calls.

North Naples is close to our Naples base, same-day service is typically available, and emergency calls get priority dispatch.
