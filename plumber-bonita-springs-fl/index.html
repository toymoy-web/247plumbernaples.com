A Different County, The Same Plumbing Problems

Barrier Island Salt, River Lots, And Slab Homes

Bonita Springs is a city of about 53,600 people in Lee County, incorporated for the second time on December 31, 1999. It sits on the mainland and on barrier islands, and the Imperial River runs through the downtown district before it empties into Estero Bay. Bonita Beach, on Little Hickory Island, is reached by road along Bonita Beach Road and Hickory Boulevard.

Those settings drive the calls. Beach-side homes and condos see the same salt-laden air that corrodes fittings on Marco Island. Lots near the river and the bay deal with high groundwater and drainage after heavy rain. Inland subdivisions east of US 41 are typical Southwest Florida slab homes, where supply lines run under or through concrete.

Know Your ZIP

Coverage Across 34134, 34135, 34133, And 34136

Bonita Springs has two street-delivery ZIP codes, 34134 and 34135. The 34134 area includes Bonita Beach and the coastal side, crosses the county line into Collier County, and holds about 24,000 residents. The 34135 area covers much of the inland side of the city, including the area around Bonita Springs High School on Imperial Parkway.

ZIP codes 34133 and 34136 are PO Box ZIPs enclosed within those two areas. We treat all four as one service area, along with the bordering ZIPs on the Naples side, including 34108 and 34110.

Water Utility

What Comes Out Of The Tap In Bonita Springs

Most of Bonita Springs is served by Bonita Springs Utilities, a not-for-profit, member-owned utility founded in 1970 that also serves the Village of Estero and parts of unincorporated south Lee County. It draws raw water from the shallow Lower Tamiami Aquifer, which is treated by lime softening, and from the deeper Upper Floridan Aquifer, which is treated by reverse osmosis. The two streams are blended.

That is a different supply from the private wells common in eastern Collier County, so we do not assume a softener or a filter is the answer. We test first, then recommend only what the water calls for. Our water softener installation and water filtration installation pages explain the options.

Permits And Counties

The Right Permit Office Depends On The Address

Bonita Springs straddles a county line. The permitting office can be the City of Bonita Springs, Lee County, or Collier County depending on the exact address, and we confirm which one applies before pulling a permit.

Work that needs a permit, such as a water heater replacement, follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), no matter which office issues it, and the paperwork is part of the job.

Real Local Detail

Old 41 Road, The Imperial River, And Barefoot Beach

Old 41 Road, signed as County Road 887, loops through historic Bonita Springs and crosses the Imperial River. The Bonita Springs Historical Society leads walking tours of sites such as the Liles Hotel. Older buildings along it often hide older plumbing, so we plan repairs around what is behind the wall and not what the drawings say.

To the west, Barefoot Beach Preserve Park covers 342 acres on the Lee and Collier County border. Hurricane Charley made landfall north of the city in 2004 and Hurricane Wilma came ashore south of it in 2005, so storm season is a normal part of planning here.

Common Questions

Bonita Springs FAQ

Do you cover every ZIP code in Bonita Springs?

Yes, we cover 34134 and 34135, the two street-delivery ZIPs, along with the 34133 and 34136 PO Box ZIPs, from Bonita Beach and Old 41 Road to the neighborhoods around Imperial Parkway.

Is emergency service available in Bonita Springs?

Yes, our emergency line dispatches to Bonita Springs 24/7, every day of the year, from our Naples base.

Do you work in Lee County as well as Collier County?

Yes. Bonita Springs is in Lee County, and we confirm the right permit office for each address before any permitted work starts.

Do I need a water softener on Bonita Springs Utilities water?

Not automatically. The utility blends lime-softened and reverse-osmosis water, so we test your water first and only recommend a system if the results call for one.

Does salt air affect plumbing near Bonita Beach?

Yes. Outdoor fittings, hose bibs, and water heater connections near the Gulf corrode faster than inland ones, so older exterior parts are worth checking.

Can you find a slab leak without cutting the floor?

Usually, yes. We locate it with acoustic and thermal equipment first, then plan the smallest repair that fixes it.

How far is Bonita Springs from your Naples base?

About 15 to 18 miles north, usually 25 to 30 minutes by US 41 or I-75, depending on traffic.
