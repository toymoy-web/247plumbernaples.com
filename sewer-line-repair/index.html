Why This Happens Here

Older Lines, Root Growth, And Shifting Soil

Naples' older neighborhoods have a mix of clay and cast iron sewer laterals, and both develop small joint gaps over decades that mature tree roots find and exploit. Sandy soil that shifts over time can also cause a section of pipe to settle unevenly, creating a low spot, called a belly, where waste collects instead of draining. Either problem shows up first as a repeat clog, not an obvious break.

Signs To Watch For

When It's The Line, Not Just A Clog

🔁

Repeat Backups

The same drain or the whole house backing up more than once in a year points to the line itself.

🌱

Soggy Or Sunken Yard Patch

A low, wet, or unusually green area along the line's path can mean it's leaking underground.

🦠

Sewage Odor Outdoors

A persistent smell near where the lateral runs is worth investigating before it becomes a backup.

Repair Options

Spot Repair, Trenchless Lining, Or Full Replacement

A single crack or offset joint often only needs a spot repair at that exact location, found and confirmed with a camera first. A line with root intrusion at multiple points may be a better fit for trenchless pipe lining, which rehabilitates the pipe from inside without full excavation. A severely deteriorated or heavily bellied line sometimes needs full replacement. We'll show you the camera footage and explain which category your line falls into before quoting.

Related Services

How We Confirm Before We Repair

Every sewer line repair starts with a sewer camera inspection so you see exactly what we see before agreeing to anything. If the immediate problem is just a clog rather than a structural issue with the line, drain cleaning or hydro jetting may resolve it without any excavation at all.

Installed To Code

Real Standards, Not Guesswork

Sewer line repairs in Florida follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which sets requirements for pipe slope, material, and joint standards. A repair that doesn't maintain the correct slope can create a new low spot even while fixing the original problem, which is why we verify slope with the camera after the repair, not just before it.

Permits are required for sewer line repair work in Collier County, and we pull those as part of the job. A repair completed without the required permit and final inspection can complicate a future home sale, when the lack of documentation raises questions a buyer's inspector will flag.

Naples-Specific Factor

Root Intrusion From Established Landscaping

Naples' older, established neighborhoods often have mature oaks and other large trees planted decades ago, sometimes close to where the original sewer lateral was run. Root systems are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside a sewer line and will exploit even a small joint gap over time, which is why root intrusion shows up more often in these established areas than in newer construction with modern PVC lines and root barriers.

Where Sewer Lines Fail

Roots In Old Naples, Age In Royal Harbor

Older neighborhoods like Old Naples, Royal Harbor, and Naples Park have mature trees and sewer laterals that have been in the ground for decades. Roots and settling are the standard causes of a cracked or offset lateral, and the first sign is usually a slow drain in the lowest fixture or a recurring clog. Naples Park falls inside North Naples.

Canal and bay-front streets in Aqualane Shores and Port Royal add a high water table, which makes digging harder and makes a camera inspection worth doing before anything is excavated. Newer areas such as Lely Resort have younger lines, so a sewer problem there is less likely to be age and more likely to be a single bad joint.

Backups Cannot Wait

Emergency Sewer Backup: Sewage In The Shower, Tub, Or Floor Drain

A sewer backup is an emergency. If sewage is coming up in a shower, a tub, or a floor drain, or if several fixtures gurgle and drain slowly at once, the main line is blocked or damaged, and the water has nowhere to go but back into the home.

Stop using water in the house. Flushing, laundry, and dishwashing all send more into the same blocked line. Keep children and pets away from the affected area, and call our 24/7 line so we can find out whether the blockage is in the house line, at the cleanout, or farther toward the street.

Pumped Systems

Sewage Ejector Pumps And Lower-Level Bathrooms

Some homes and condos use a sewage ejector pump to lift waste from a lower bathroom or basement-level fixture up to the main line. When that pump fails, the bathroom below floods, and it can happen at any hour.

We replace failed ejector pumps and check the float, the check valve, and the discharge line, because a new pump on a clogged discharge will fail again. If a lift pump is not behaving, say so when you call so the right pump is on the truck.

Lasting Fix

When The Backup Is A Damaged Line, Not A Clog

Clearing a blockage is not the same as fixing a line. After the backup is cleared, we run a camera when it makes sense, so you can see whether roots, a belly, or a break caused it.

If the line needs repair, we explain the options in plain language, from a spot repair to trenchless lining to full replacement, and the likely cost range for each before any work begins.

Common Questions

Sewer Line FAQ

Do I need to see the camera footage before agreeing to a repair?

We show it to you as standard practice, not just on request, so you understand exactly what's being fixed and why.

Will my yard be torn up for the repair?

Not necessarily. Spot repairs and trenchless lining both minimize excavation compared to a full traditional dig, which we'll only recommend when it's genuinely the right fix.

How long does trenchless lining take?

Most residential lining jobs are completed in a single day once the camera inspection and prep work are done.

Can tree roots grow back into a repaired line?

If the underlying joint gap isn't addressed, yes. Trenchless lining creates a continuous new interior surface that roots can't easily re-enter, which is part of why we recommend it for root-related repeat problems.

How do I know if I need a spot repair or a full replacement?

The camera inspection tells us. A single confirmed defect usually means a spot repair. Multiple defects or a badly bellied section across a longer run usually points toward replacement or full-length lining, and we'll explain which category your line falls into with the footage in hand.

Is sewage backing up into the house an emergency?

Yes. Stop using water, keep people and pets out of the area, and call our 24/7 line. Sewage in living space is a health issue as well as a plumbing one.

Do you do trenchless sewer lateral replacement?

Trenchless options are part of the sewer line conversation. Whether it fits depends on the pipe's condition, the depth, and what a camera inspection shows, so we confirm that first.

Do you replace sewage ejector pumps?

Yes. We replace ejector and lift pumps and check the float, check valve, and discharge line at the same time.

What makes a sewer problem an emergency instead of a scheduled repair?

Sewage in a tub, shower, or floor drain, or several fixtures backing up together. A slow drain with no backup can usually be booked, while an emergency backup gets dispatched on our 24/7 line.
