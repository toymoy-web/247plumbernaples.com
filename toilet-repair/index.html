Common Problems

What Usually Goes Wrong

🔄

Won't Stop Running

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve, one of the simplest and least expensive fixes we do.

💧

Leaking At The Base

Often a failed wax ring seal between the toilet and the floor flange.

🌀

Clogs Repeatedly

Can be the toilet itself, the trap design, or a partial blockage further down the line.

↔️

Wobbles When You Sit

Usually loose or corroded floor bolts, sometimes combined with a damaged flange underneath.

Repair Or Replace

When A New Toilet Makes More Sense

A toilet more than 15 to 20 years old, especially an older high-flow model, often costs more to keep repairing than a modern low-flow unit costs to install, once you factor in the water savings over time. A cracked tank or bowl means replacement is the only real option regardless of age. For a toilet under 10 years old with a single, isolated problem, repair is almost always the better value.

Naples-Specific Factor

Wax Rings And Slab Foundations

Most Naples homes have toilets mounted directly on a concrete slab rather than a wood subfloor, which means the flange sits at floor level with less room for adjustment than a framed floor allows. A settling slab over decades can shift the flange's height slightly, which is a common, often-overlooked reason a toilet rocks even after floor bolts are tightened. We check flange condition as part of any wobble complaint, not just the bolts.

Installed To Code

Water Efficiency Standards

New toilet installations in Florida must meet the water-efficiency standards set by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, generally 1.6 gallons per flush or less for standard models, with high-efficiency models using considerably less. This isn't just a preference, it's the code requirement for new installations, and we make sure any replacement toilet meets it rather than installing an older, non-compliant model even if a customer happens to have one on hand.

Choosing A Replacement

Comfort Height, Bowl Shape, And Flush Type

Comfort-height toilets sit a couple of inches taller than standard models and have become the common default in new installations, generally easier for most adults to use. Elongated bowls offer more seating room than round bowls but need slightly more clearance in a small bathroom. Pressure-assist flush mechanisms clear waste more forcefully than gravity-fed models, worth considering for a household with a history of clogs, though they run slightly louder. We'll walk through what fits your specific bathroom rather than defaulting to one style.

Related Services

If The Problem Is Bigger Than The Toilet

A toilet that clogs repeatedly even after a fresh install sometimes points to a problem further down the line rather than the fixture itself. If that's the case, we'll recommend a sewer camera inspection rather than replacing the toilet again for no reason. And if a running toilet has been going unnoticed for a while, it's worth a broader look with our general plumbing repair service to check for related issues.

Bathrooms We Service

Older Toilets In Naples Park, New Fixtures In Lely Resort

Running toilets, weak flushes, and rocking bases are the standard calls. In older homes in Naples Park, Naples Manor, and Old Naples, a wobbling toilet often means a failed wax ring or a damaged flange, and the floor underneath is what we check first. We cover Naples Manor as part of East Naples.

Comfort-height toilets are a common upgrade for homeowners planning to stay put, in Lely, Pelican Bay, and Park Shore alike, and installation is straightforward when the flange is sound. Common Questions

Toilet Repair FAQ

My toilet rocks slightly when I sit down. Is that serious?

It's worth addressing before it gets worse, since a rocking toilet can eventually break the wax ring seal and cause a leak at the base.

How long does a toilet installation take?

Most installations, including removal of the old toilet, take under two hours.

Are low-flow toilets actually worth it?

For most households, yes. Modern low-flow toilets use significantly less water per flush than older models while flushing just as effectively, which adds up on the water bill over time.

Can you match my existing toilet's bolt pattern and height?

In most cases yes. We'll check the rough-in measurement before ordering a replacement to make sure it fits your existing setup.

How do I know if I need a new wax ring or a new toilet entirely?

If the toilet itself is in good condition and just leaking at the base, a wax ring replacement usually resolves it. We'll confirm the bowl and tank aren't cracked before recommending the simpler fix.

Is it normal for a toilet to need a new flapper every year or two?

Flappers typically last several years, so needing one annually often points to water quality issues degrading the rubber faster than normal, worth mentioning when you call.
