Why It Drips

The Real Cost Of Ignoring A Drip

A faucet dripping once per second wastes several gallons a day, which adds up to a noticeable amount on a monthly water bill over time. Beyond the cost, a persistent drip in a bathroom sink or tub can also stain porcelain over months, a cosmetic problem that's harder to reverse than the drip itself was to fix. Most drip repairs are quick, inexpensive, and worth doing as soon as you notice the problem rather than waiting.

Naples-Specific Factor

Why Fixtures Wear Faster Here

Hard water, more common in well-water areas like Golden Gate Estates and Immokalee but present to a lesser degree on municipal supply too, accelerates mineral buildup inside faucet cartridges and valves. That buildup is what eventually causes drips, stuck handles, and reduced flow at aerators. A whole-home water softener can meaningfully slow this process across every fixture in the house, not just the one currently giving you trouble.

Choosing A Replacement

What To Consider Beyond Looks

Beyond style, faucet quality varies meaningfully by valve type. Ceramic disc valves last longer and resist mineral buildup better than older compression or ball-valve designs, worth the modest price difference for most households. We can talk through options in your budget rather than just installing whatever's cheapest, since a faucet that fails again in two years costs more in the long run than one that holds up.

Sink Materials

What Holds Up In A Naples Kitchen

Stainless steel remains the most durable everyday choice for kitchen sinks and resists chipping in a way that porcelain and enamel-coated cast iron don't. Composite granite sinks offer more color options and resist scratching well but can show hard-water mineral spotting more visibly than stainless in areas with harder water. We'll talk through what fits your kitchen's actual use rather than just its appearance.

Bathroom Fixtures

Bathroom Faucets Have Different Demands

Bathroom faucets see less heavy use than kitchen faucets but are exposed to soap residue and toothpaste buildup around the base that can accelerate wear on the finish over time. A vanity sink's drain assembly, the pop-up stopper mechanism specifically, is also a common source of slow drainage that's separate from the trap clog issues more typical in kitchen sinks.

We service both as part of the same visit when a bathroom has more than one fixture issue at once, which is common once a fixture starts showing its age.

Faucets And Sinks By Neighborhood

Hard-Water Scale, Salt Air, And Older Valves

Naples' hard water leaves mineral scale in aerators and cartridges, so low flow and dripping are the top faucet calls from Old Naples to East Naples. A cleaning or a cartridge swap is often enough, and we say so when it is. The same goes for Bonita Springs, just across the county line.

Near the Gulf and Naples Bay, in Park Shore, The Moorings, and Royal Harbor, salt air corrodes exposed finishes and supply lines faster, so the fix often includes replacing the stops and supply lines at the same time. Older shutoff valves under sinks sometimes fail when turned, which is worth knowing before a repair.

Failed Valves

Emergency Calls: Broken Supply Valves And Water That Will Not Shut Off

A leaking faucet can wait a day. A snapped supply valve, a tub that will not shut off, or a valve that sprays when it is turned cannot. Those are the faucet and valve calls we treat as emergencies.

If a stop valve under a sink or behind a toilet fails, close the main shutoff at the house and call our 24/7 line. Closing the main stops the water everywhere while we replace the valve and, if needed, the supply line.

Showers And Fixtures

Shower Valves, Tub Spouts, And Cartridge Replacement

A shower valve that drips, a tub spout that leaks from the wall, or a handle that will not hold temperature usually needs a new cartridge. When a leak shows up inside the wall behind the shower, we check whether it is the valve body or the supply line before deciding what to open.

We replace faucets as well as repair them, and we price the replacement separately from the service call so you can compare. Ask us for the fixture price when you call.

Common Questions

Faucet & Sink FAQ

My faucet just started dripping. Is that urgent?

Not an emergency, but worth scheduling soon since it wastes water and can worsen the longer it's ignored.

Can you install a faucet I bought myself?

Yes, we regularly install customer-supplied fixtures. Just confirm the fixture matches your sink's existing hole configuration first.

Why is water pressure low only at my kitchen sink?

Usually a clogged aerator, the small screen at the tip of the faucet, which is often a five-minute fix rather than a bigger plumbing issue.

My sink drains slowly but isn't fully clogged. Is that worth fixing?

Yes, a slow drain usually gets worse over time rather than resolving on its own, and catching it early is quicker and less expensive than waiting for a full blockage.

Can you replace a sink without replacing the countertop?

In most cases yes, as long as the new sink fits the existing cutout or the cutout can be reasonably modified. We'll assess this before recommending a specific replacement or any countertop work.

What's causing the gurgling sound when my sink drains?

Usually a venting issue or a partial blockage further down the line creating an air pocket. It's worth having checked since it can be an early sign of a developing clog.

Is a broken supply valve an emergency?

If water is spraying or will not shut off, yes. Close the main shutoff at the house and call our 24/7 line.

Can you install new outdoor faucets?

Yes. We install and replace outdoor spigots, including new faucets run from inside the house, and we check for leaks on the supply line.

When is a faucet or valve problem an emergency?

It is an emergency when water is spraying, a valve will not close, or a leak is reaching a floor or cabinet. A slow drip can wait for a scheduled visit, but an emergency call to our 24/7 line gets the water stopped first.
