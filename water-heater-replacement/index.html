Signs It's Time

When Replacement Beats Another Repair

📅

Past 8 To 10 Years Old

Once a tank is past this age, a second or third repair rarely makes financial sense.

💧

Leaking At The Base

A leak from the tank itself, not a fitting, means the tank is failing internally.

🦠

Rusty Or Metallic-Tasting Water

Often a sign the tank's interior lining has broken down.

📉

Rising Energy Bills

An aging unit loses efficiency well before it fails outright.

Install Standards

Done To Code, Not Just Done

Every installation follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, including required expansion tanks, proper venting for gas units, and code-compliant drain pan placement. Skipping these on a replacement is a common shortcut that shows up later as a failed inspection at resale or, worse, an actual safety issue. We install to code the first time.

And if you're on the fence about switching to tankless, our tankless water heater page walks through the real trade-offs.

Sizing It Right

Getting The Capacity Right The First Time

An undersized tank means running out of hot water during normal household use, while an oversized one wastes energy keeping water hot that never gets used. We size the replacement to your household's actual fixture count and peak usage, not just a like-for-like swap of whatever was there before, since a home's hot water demand can change over the years a previous unit was installed. A household that's added a bathroom or grown in size since the original installation often needs a genuinely larger unit, not the same capacity replaced automatically.

Naples-Specific Note

Garage Installations And Flood Elevation

Many Naples homes keep the water heater in the garage, and in flood-prone areas of Collier County that can mean an elevation requirement for the unit above the garage floor. We check this as part of every replacement so the new installation meets current code, not just matches what was there before, which may have predated the current requirement.

Electric Vs. Gas Vs. Heat Pump

Choosing A Fuel Type

Electric tank units are the simplest to install and the most common default when no gas line already runs to the water heater location. Gas units heat faster and typically cost less to run where natural gas or propane is already available. Heat pump water heaters use ambient air to move heat rather than generating it directly, which makes them notably efficient in Naples' warm climate, though they need adequate air space around the unit to work well and cost more upfront. We'll walk through what your home already has and what switching would actually involve before you decide.

Replacement Jobs By Neighborhood

Permits, Condo Rules, And Tight Closets

Replacing a water heater is permitted work, and in Pelican Bay, Park Shore, and Vanderbilt Beach the building association often adds its own rules on top, from approved delivery hours to insurance paperwork. We confirm those requirements before the old tank comes out so the swap does not stall. The Pelican Bay and Vanderbilt Beach buildings are covered under North Naples.

In older single-family areas like Naples Park, Old Naples, and Royal Harbor, access is usually easier but code updates can be harder, since venting, pan drains, and expansion tanks may need to be brought up to current standards. Tell us the neighborhood on the first call and we will plan the day around it.

Common Questions

Replacement FAQ

Can you replace my water heater the same day I call?

In most cases, yes, assuming the unit is in stock for your home's configuration. We'll confirm timing when you call.

Do you haul away the old unit?

Yes, removal and disposal of the old water heater is included with a replacement.

Is switching from a tank to tankless a same-day job?

Usually not, since it often requires new venting or gas line work. We'll give you an accurate timeline once we see your setup.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Naples?

Most jurisdictions in Collier County require a permit for water heater replacement. We handle the permitting as part of the job.

Is a heat pump water heater worth the extra cost in Florida?

For many households, yes, since Naples' warm climate is close to ideal for how these units work. The payback period depends on your household's hot water usage and current energy costs, which we can walk through for your specific situation.
