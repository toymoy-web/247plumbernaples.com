Home Zone

The City Of Naples And The Beach Districts

Naples proper runs on municipal water and sewer, with a housing stock ranging from decades-old homes in Old Naples to newer construction further inland, and slab foundations throughout that make slab leaks a recurring local issue.

Inside the city we work the whole beach district: Old Naples around the Naples Pier, Third Street South, and Fifth Avenue South, the canal neighborhoods of Aqualane Shores, Port Royal, and Royal Harbor on Naples Bay, and The Moorings and Park Shore along the Gulf. These neighborhoods share one water system and one housing history, so they are covered on our homepage and not on separate pages.

Dispatch By Area

How Emergency Dispatch Works Across The Service Area

Our dispatch begins in Naples, so response times follow distance. The home zone and the nearby communities of North Naples and East Naples are the quickest to reach. Golden Gate and Marco Island come next, then Bonita Springs, and last the outer ring of Ave Maria and Immokalee.

When you call, we give you a realistic arrival window for your address instead of a vague promise. Active leaks, sewage backups, and homes with no water go first in the queue, wherever they are.

If your problem is not an emergency, we schedule the visit for a time that suits you and tell you what it will cover. Describing the problem on the phone helps us bring the right parts and equipment on the first trip.

What To Tell Us

What To Say When You Call From Any Area

Give us your address or nearest cross street, whether you are on city water or a private well, and whether the home is on sewer or septic. Those details change what we bring. A well home with no water points to a pump or a pressure tank, while a city-water home points to a main or a meter-side problem.

Also tell us what you can see. A wet ceiling, a running meter, a gurgling toilet, or a heater leaking onto a garage floor each calls for different equipment.

If you can safely reach the main shutoff, close it before we arrive. If you cannot find it, say so, and we help you locate it over the phone.

Before You Call

Knowing Your Water And Sewer Setup Saves Time

Homeowners across the service area sit on very different systems. Some are on city water and sewer, some on private wells and septic, and some on utilities that are neither the city nor the county. If you are unsure, check a recent bill or ask the utility.

Knowing your setup lets us plan the visit, which matters most when the call is urgent and the home is a long drive from our base. It also tells us whether the problem could be on the utility side of the meter or on yours, so we send the right person with the right equipment the first time.
