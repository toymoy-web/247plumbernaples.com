Small Problems, Real Cost

Why A Small Leak Is Worth Fixing Now

A toilet that runs continuously can waste hundreds of gallons a day, showing up as a real jump on the water bill long before anyone notices standing water anywhere. A slow drip under a sink can quietly damage cabinetry over months. Most general repair calls are inexpensive precisely because we catch them before they turn into something bigger, which is the whole reason it's worth calling about something that seems minor.

Related Services

When It's More Than A Quick Fix

If a diagnostic visit turns up something more significant, a hidden leak, a failing water heater, or a fixture that needs full replacement, we'll walk you through the next step rather than just patching around it. That might mean leak detection, toilet replacement, or faucet repair depending on what we actually find.

Done To Code

Every Repair Follows The Florida Building Code, Plumbing

Even a small repair, a new shutoff valve or a replaced fixture, affects how the rest of the system performs, which is why we install to the Florida Building Code, Plumbing standard rather than treating small jobs as exempt from doing it right. A valve that's the wrong size or a fitting that's not properly sealed can create a bigger problem down the line, even when the immediate symptom is fixed.

Naples-Specific Note

Older Fixtures And Hard Water

Fixtures in Naples homes, especially those on well water in outlying areas, tend to accumulate mineral deposits faster than fixtures on treated municipal water.

A cartridge or valve that would last many years elsewhere can wear out sooner here, which is part of why a "small" repair sometimes recurs faster than a homeowner expects, and why we check the water source as part of understanding the real cause.

If the same fixture keeps failing, treating the water quality issue directly is often more cost-effective long-term than repeatedly replacing the same part.

Ceiling And Wall Leaks

Water Coming Through A Ceiling Or Wall

A stain, a sag, or a hole in a ceiling usually means a leak from the bathroom above, a supply line in the attic, or a drain that has failed. In older homes, S-traps and worn drain connections can also let water through slowly.

Do not poke at a bulging ceiling unless you are ready for the water, and place a bucket under it. Turn off the water to the room above if you can, or close the main, and call us. We find the source, repair it, and tell you what needs drying so the damage does not spread.

Pressure And Supply

Low Water Pressure, Or No Water At The House

Weak pressure at every faucet suggests a supply problem such as a failing pressure regulator, a partly closed valve, or a corroded line. If the pressure is only low at one fixture, the cause is usually local, like a clogged aerator or a worn cartridge.

No water at all is a different problem. A bill that is paid, a neighbor with water, and a quiet meter point toward your side. If a well pressure tank has failed, the whole system stops, and we cover that on the well pump page.

Leak Repairs, Bathroom Leaks, And Pipe Work Behind The Wall

General repair covers small leaks in supply lines, bathroom leaks that soak into the floor, and reconnection work after a remodel. We diagnose first, so you know what the problem is, what the fix involves, and what it costs before work starts.

Common Questions

General Repair FAQ

My toilet keeps running. Is that worth calling about?

Yes, it's one of the most common and least expensive calls we get, and it's actively costing you money on your water bill every day it continues.

Do you charge just to come look at a problem?

We charge a diagnostic visit fee for non-emergency calls, which is credited toward the repair if you move forward with the work.

Can you fix something even if I don't know what's wrong?

Yes, describing the symptom is enough to get started. We'll diagnose the actual cause on site.

Why does the same fixture keep needing repair?

Repeated failures on the same fixture often point to water quality rather than a defective part. We'll check for hard water contribution before simply replacing the same component again.

Do you work on both older and newer homes?

Yes, our general repair work covers everything from decades-old fixtures to recently built homes, and the diagnostic approach is the same either way.

What if the repair turns out to be bigger than expected?

We'll stop, explain what we found, and give you a revised price before doing any additional work, never proceed on an open-ended basis without your sign-off.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Water you cannot stop, sewage backing up, a burst pipe, a flooding water heater, or no water to the house. Anything that is actively damaging your home is worth calling our 24/7 line for.

Can you give a price before you arrive?

We can talk through the likely range over the phone once you describe the problem, and we confirm the price after we see it, before any work begins.
