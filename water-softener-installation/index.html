Related Services

Softeners And Your Water Heater

Sediment from hard water is one of the most common reasons a water heater needs repair or early replacement in this area. Installing a softener alongside a new unit is one of the more effective ways to extend its life, and homes on private wells in Golden Gate Estates tend to see the most benefit.

Well Water Vs. City Water

Why Testing Comes First

Naples' municipal water is treated and monitored, so hardness is generally consistent and predictable. Private wells across Golden Gate Estates, Immokalee, and Ave Maria vary property to property depending on depth and the local aquifer, and can carry iron or sulfur in addition to hardness minerals. We test the actual water before recommending a system size, rather than assuming municipal-level hardness applies to a well.

What's Included

Installation And What Comes After

A standard softener installation includes connecting the unit to your main water line, setting the correct regeneration schedule for your household size and water hardness, and testing the output afterward to confirm it's actually softening correctly. Salt-based systems need periodic salt refills, which we'll walk you through, along with a realistic sense of how often that's needed for your specific setup.

Salt-Based Vs. Salt-Free

Which Type Fits Your Household

Salt-based softeners are the traditional, most effective option for genuinely hard water, removing minerals through an ion-exchange process. Salt-free systems condition water rather than truly softening it, using less maintenance but offering a milder effect, better suited to moderately hard water than the harder well water common in outlying Collier County. We'll recommend based on your actual test results, not a default assumption either way.

Sizing The System

Grain Capacity And Household Size

Softeners are rated by grain capacity, how many grains of hardness the system can remove before it needs to regenerate. Sizing depends on household size, actual water hardness in grains per gallon, and daily water usage, not just the number of bathrooms in the home. An undersized unit regenerates too often and wears out faster.

An oversized one costs more upfront than the household actually needs. We test your water and size accordingly rather than defaulting to a single standard unit for every install.

Where It Goes

Placement In A Naples Home

Most installations go in the garage where the main water line enters the home, close to the existing plumbing and with enough clearance for salt refills and maintenance access. For homes without garage space at that point, we can route the installation to a utility closet or exterior location with proper weatherproofing, which is worth discussing during the initial visit rather than assuming garage placement is the only option.

Where Hard Water Hits Hardest

Well Homes In The Estates And City Water In Pelican Bay

Naples has hard water, and the scale shows up as white crust on faucets, cloudy glass, and shortened water heater life. The effect can be strongest on private wells, which are common across Golden Gate Estates, where mineral content is often higher than on city supply.

In Pelican Bay, Park Shore, and the golf communities of Lely Resort and Mediterra, we test first, because a whole-house softener is a bigger decision in a building or a community with association rules about plumbing changes. Across the county line in Bonita Springs, the utility blends softened and reverse-osmosis water, which changes the answer again.

Repairs And Installs

Repairs, Valves, And Installing Equipment You Already Bought

Not everyone starts from scratch. We hear from homeowners who already bought the softener and need it installed, from owners whose unit stopped regenerating, and from people whose softener valve broke in a move.

We repair control valves, check the brine tank, and retrieve objects dropped into the brine well. If the unit is a few years old and failing, we tell you whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.

Common Questions

Water Softener FAQ

Do I need a different system for well water versus city water?

Often yes. Well water can carry additional minerals or iron that affect which system size and type fit best, which we'll test for before recommending equipment.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed in half a day.

My softener stopped regenerating. Is that a simple fix?

Often yes, commonly a control valve or timer issue rather than a full system failure. We diagnose before recommending replacement.

How often does a softener need salt refills?

It depends on household size and water hardness, typically every four to eight weeks for an average household, which we'll estimate for your actual usage.

Will a softener remove iron from well water?

A standard softener handles low levels of iron, but well water with significant iron content usually needs a dedicated iron filter installed ahead of the softener, which we'll test for and recommend if needed since iron left untreated can also stain fixtures and laundry over time.

Can you install a water softener I bought myself?

Yes. Bring the model details and we check the bypass, drain connection, and electrical, then install it to the manufacturer's requirements.
