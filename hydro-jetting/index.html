Snaking Didn't Fix It?

Why A Repeat Clog Needs A Different Approach

A snake punches a hole through a clog, clearing the immediate blockage but leaving grease, scale, and root hair coating the pipe walls behind. That residue is exactly what the next clog forms around. Hydro jetting removes the buildup itself, not just the blockage, which is why it holds up longer against repeat clogs in the same section of pipe.

How It Works

The Process

A specialized nozzle feeds through a cleanout and sprays water at pressures typically between 1,500 and 4,000 PSI, depending on the pipe material and the severity of the buildup. The nozzle moves both forward and backward through the line, and the reverse-facing jets scour the pipe walls clean as it travels. We always run a camera inspection first to confirm the pipe's condition and material can handle jetting safely.

Is It Safe For My Pipes?

When We Recommend It, And When We Don't

Jetting is safe and effective on PVC and healthy cast iron or clay pipe. On severely deteriorated older pipe, high pressure can worsen an already-weak section, which is exactly why the camera inspection comes first. If a line isn't a good candidate for jetting, we'll say so and recommend an alternative rather than jetting anyway and risking damage.

Related Services

When Jetting Reveals A Bigger Problem

Jetting clears buildup, but it won't fix a cracked, offset, or root-intruded pipe, those need sewer line repair. If the camera inspection we run beforehand turns up a structural issue, we'll explain it clearly before recommending jetting as anything more than a temporary fix.

Real Standards

Following The Florida Building Code, Plumbing

Jetting pressure and technique follow standards under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing framework licensed plumbers work within statewide. That matters most on older or more fragile pipe, where the wrong pressure or technique could cause damage rather than clean the line, which is exactly why we assess pipe condition first rather than jetting at a single default setting regardless of what's actually there.

Preventive Use

Jetting Before There's A Problem

Some Naples homeowners, particularly those with mature trees near known sewer line paths or a kitchen that sees heavy cooking use, schedule jetting on a preventive basis every year or two rather than waiting for a clog. This costs less over time than repeated emergency clog calls and catches root intrusion or buildup before it causes an actual backup.

Commercial Properties

Grease Line Maintenance

Restaurants and commercial kitchens in the Naples area typically need line jetting on a regular schedule, often quarterly, to stay ahead of grease buildup that accumulates faster in commercial settings than in a residential kitchen.

Falling behind on this schedule is one of the more common causes of an emergency commercial backup, which tends to happen at the worst possible time, during service. We can set up a recurring maintenance schedule for commercial accounts rather than waiting for a call after something backs up, and can typically work around service hours to minimize disruption.

Where Jetting Is Used

Restaurant Rows, Condo Stacks, And Root-Bound Laterals

Hydro jetting is the right tool for grease and scale in commercial kitchen lines, so it comes up around the restaurants of Fifth Avenue South, Third Street South, and Mercato in North Naples. It is also the better choice for a main line that keeps clogging in older neighborhoods like Naples Park, where roots and buildup return after a snake.

In condo buildings in Pelican Bay and Park Shore, jetting a shared stack on a schedule can prevent the all-at-once backups that flood ground-floor units. We run a camera first on any older line so the pressure is matched to the pipe.

Comparing Quotes

Getting A Quote For Hydro Jetting

Callers often tell us they are collecting a couple of quotes for hydro jetting before they decide. That is reasonable, and the useful comparison is not just the price. Ask what pressure the jetter runs at, whether a camera is used before and after, and whether the visit covers the full line or only the first stretch.

Describe where the clog shows up, how often it returns, and what kind of building it is. That lets us give you a realistic range before we arrive.

Floor Drains And Commercial Lines

Floor Drains, Grease-Clogged Lines, And Commercial Drains

Hydro jetting is also the right tool for grease-packed kitchen lines, silted floor drains, and long commercial runs. When a floor drain overflows or a steel grate has to come off first, tell us before the visit so the crew brings what it needs.

Jetting clears the line walls instead of punching a hole through the blockage, which is why it holds up longer in restaurant and multi-unit drains.

Common Questions

Hydro Jetting FAQ

Is hydro jetting better than snaking?

For a repeat clog in the same location, yes, since it removes buildup rather than just punching through it. For a first-time, isolated clog, snaking is often sufficient and less expensive.

Can hydro jetting damage old pipes?

It can on severely deteriorated pipe, which is why we always inspect with a camera first and only recommend jetting when the pipe can safely handle it.

How often should a line be jetted?

It depends on usage and pipe age. A line with a history of grease buildup might benefit from jetting every couple of years as preventive maintenance.

Will jetting fix a slow drain that's never fully clogged?

Yes, jetting works well on a chronically slow drain even before it becomes a full blockage, and catching it at that stage is often cheaper than waiting for an emergency call.

Do I need to be home during jetting service?

Not necessarily for exterior cleanout access, though we recommend being available in case we need to discuss what the camera inspection finds before proceeding.

Can hydro jetting clear a main line that backs up repeatedly?

Often, yes, when the cause is grease, scale, or roots. A camera tells us whether the line is damaged, which jetting alone cannot fix.
